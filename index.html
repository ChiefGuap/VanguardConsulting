Engagement Timeline

The path to executable solutions.

Our consultants undergo extensive development and structured mentorship to guarantee professional-grade deliverables.

01

Multidisciplinary Selection

We recruit candidates with backgrounds in computer science, law, engineering, and economics. This diversity of thought ensures we look at strategy challenges through multiple functional lenses.

02

Rigorous Skills Training

All newly recruited consultants go through a thorough training period studying traditional business frameworks, quantitative analytics, and modern business software tools.

03

Industry Mentorship

Teams are coached and mentored directly by consultants working at top-tier global firms. This keeps our frameworks aligned with modern commercial standards.

04

10-12 Week Client Engagements

Consultants work in small, focused teams directly engaging with clients. Through intensive research, modeling, and client feedback, we deliver actionable solutions that drive measurable impact.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions.

We work with growth-stage ventures and established companies looking for technology-informed, data-driven strategic advisory. Our client engagements range from local Northern California firms to global tech companies.

We deliberately recruit across all majors at UC Davis. While many business clubs focus solely on economics, we actively look for students in computer science, engineering, law, cognitive science, and social sciences. We value analytical rigor and technical fluency.

Our client engagements last between 10 and 12 weeks, matching the UC Davis quarter system. This structure allows us to build dedicated project plans with clear, measurable deliverables by the end of the term.

All consultants go through a rigorous education period where they are trained in market analysis, business frameworks (MECE, SWOT, Porter's 5 Forces), data analytics (Excel/SQL/Python), slide deck design, and client presentation skills.
